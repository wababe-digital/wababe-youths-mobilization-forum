About Us

Game da Wababe Youths Mobilization Forum

Who We Are

Wababe Youths Mobilization Forum kungiya ce da aka kafa domin haɗa kan matasa, ƙarfafa zumunci, haɗin kai da samar da ayyukan da za su taimaka wajen ci gaban matasa da al'umma.

Our Vision

Gina matasa masu haɗin kai, ilimi, ƙwarewa, kishin al'umma da kuma shirye-shiryen taimakawa wajen ci gaban yankinmu.

Our Mission

Muna aiki tare da matasa domin samar da dama, ƙarfafa ilimi da sana'o'i, community development, empowerment da kuma haɗin kai tsakanin matasa.

Our Activities

Ayyukan Wababe Youths Mobilization Forum

🏘️ Community Development

Shirya ayyukan da za su kawo ci gaba ga al'umma.

📚 Education

Ƙarfafa ilimi da wayar da kan matasa.

🌱 Social Development

Samar da kyakkyawan tasiri a tsakanin al'umma.

🎯 Youth Forums
